Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

What we collect

Heirloom runs entirely in your browser. We do not run user accounts, we do not collect your name, email or phone number, and we do not store anything about you on a server.

Saved on your device

Your progress (money, level, outfit, settings) is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. Clearing your browser data deletes it. You can also erase it inside the game: Menu → Reset.

Third parties

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of normal font delivery (Google's privacy policy). The hosting provider (Netlify) may keep standard server logs. We use no advertising or analytics trackers.

Chat and other players

The chat and the other "players" and leaderboard in this version are simulated and run locally. Nothing you type is sent to anyone.

Children

Heirloom is intended for players aged 13 and over (16+ in some countries). It collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Parents with concerns can contact us below.

Deleting your data

Use Menu → Reset in the game, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Because we hold no data on servers, there is nothing else to delete.
